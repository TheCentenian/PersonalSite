**Who I am**

I’m a Sui ecosystem builder, product architect, and systems thinker. I build experimental apps around blockchain UX, asset ownership, and event-driven systems. I care about how users experience on-chain data, how digital assets are owned and used, and how systems can be designed so that apps and games don’t have to reinvent the wheel every time they touch the chain.

---

**What I’ve been doing**

Over the past year and a half I’ve been focused on learning and building within the Sui ecosystem. Many ideas became prototypes and MVPs. I’ve directed the end-to-end lifecycle for multiple independent projects: a multi-chain Web3 portfolio tracker, a liquidity automation tool, and several blockchain-integrated game prototypes that use smart contract logic. I design system architecture and frontend builds with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind, integrating blockchain APIs and real-time data from Sui (and in some work, Hedera). I’ve led implementation of auth and database systems—MongoDB Atlas, JWT, Render—and built automation for transaction tracking and liquidity flows with Node.js and REST/GraphQL. I use AI-assisted development (Cursor IDE) to move faster on reviews, debugging, and documentation. I’ve delivered MVPs for full-stack blockchain products with modular architecture designed for scalability, and I’ve been invited to contribute to the CTO team of a Sui-based token project, where I’ve helped with technical coordination, operations planning, and partnership management. I’m now sharing some of the more complete systems I’ve engineered—including shared infrastructure for apps and games (Aqueduct), goal and transaction-tracking tools (Evarra Tracker), and a blockchain-integrated game (SuiTwo Market Shooter).

---

**Background**

I hold a Master of Science in Information Technology from Southern New Hampshire University and a Bachelor of Arts in Communication with a minor in Business Administration from the University of New Hampshire. I’m certified in ITIL Foundation V3 and ITIL Foundation 4.

Before shifting to Web3, I spent six years as Service Delivery Manager at Retail Business Services (Ahold Delhaize), where I managed enterprise IT service delivery across supply chain, finance, and corporate systems supporting over 200 applications and platforms. I led cross-functional technical teams during large-scale modernization initiatives—warehouse onboarding, new facility integrations, distribution center launches—and served as primary escalation lead for major incidents, coordinating between internal support, vendors, and leadership. I chaired ITIL governance committees (Change, Problem, CMDB), improved incident response and CMDB accuracy through process and automation, and produced executive-level performance reports and dashboards. That experience gave me a strong foundation in systems thinking, stakeholder coordination, and keeping complex environments stable while they change.
